- **Need:** Identify churn-associated attributes and groups for retention investigation.
- **Approach:** JMP data review, recoding/imputation, nominal logistic model, odds-ratio and classification review.
- **Result:** Training accuracy **93.79%**, but churn recall **0%**; every churn case is missed in the saved matrix.
- **Value:** Reveals validation and data-quality gaps; no retention lift was measured.
